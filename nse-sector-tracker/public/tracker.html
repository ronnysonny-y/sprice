A clearer view of the market.

Choose a sector, select a period, then compare up to 15 stocks ranked by total market capitalisation from the latest available NSE daily report.

Universe & classification

For the 22 sectors, we use the current Nifty Total Market constituent list, not every listed NSE security. The 32 choices include NSE’s 22 sectors and 10 industry groups. The 10 industry groups use named official Nifty index constituent lists, shown on the results page; they do not include every company in that industry. A group can contain fewer than 15 eligible companies.

Prices & percentage changes

Closing prices come from NSE historical equity data, in ₹ and as reported (not adjusted for corporate actions). Daily change compares a close with the immediately preceding trading session, including sessions omitted from the display. Period change compares the first and last available closes within your selected range.

A maximum of 30 prices

The sampling interval is ⌈calendar days ÷ 30⌉ trading sessions, increased if needed to stay within 30 dates. The latest available close is always included. Weekends and exchange holidays have no closing price. A 1-day view shows the latest completed session.

Official data access

NSE may restrict automated requests. We never replace missing prices with invented data. The site reads official historical equity prices and daily market-cap reports. Requests are cached briefly and can be retried when NSE is unavailable.

For research and comparison. Rankings reflect the current universe, not historical constituents.
